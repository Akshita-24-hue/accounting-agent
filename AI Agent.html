      Import a bank statement

      Choose your bank statement (Excel or CSV, or a photo of one), drag it onto the box, or paste the text straight from your bank. The app works out the columns for you.

      GST invoice

      Upload a photo or screenshot (PNG, JPG, WebP) and Claude fills in the fields, or type them yourself. Every field is checked as you edit. Always compare with the original invoice.

        Financial model and valuation

        Starts from your books, projects five years, and values the business three ways: discounted cash flow (DCF), an EBITDA multiple and a P/E multiple. Every assumption below is yours to change. The numbers are illustrative, not a formal valuation report.

        Quick setup: three questions

        Tap one answer in each row. Your income and costs come from your books automatically. These are typical starting values, not facts about your business, so adjust them below if you know better.
